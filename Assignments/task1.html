About ANITS

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences (ANITS) is one of the premier engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh, offering quality education in Engineering, Technology and Management.

The college encourages innovation, research, and industry-oriented learning to help students become successful professionals.
